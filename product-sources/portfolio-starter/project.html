Greenleaf Clinic booking

Redesigning appointment booking for a fictional community clinic so patients can book in under two minutes.

Role
Lead product designer
Timeline
10 weeks
Team
1 designer, 2 developers
Tools
Prototyping, usability tests

The problem

Patients had to pass through nine screens and create an account before seeing a single available time. Many gave up and phoned instead, which overloaded reception staff.

Approach

We interviewed patients and receptionists, mapped the current journey, and tested three paper prototypes before committing to a direction.

  • Show available times first; ask for details last.
  • Make accounts optional with a one-time email link.
  • Use plain language and large touch targets throughout.

Solution

A four-step flow: choose a reason, pick a time, add contact details, confirm. Each step fits on a small phone screen without scrolling.

Outcomes (placeholder figures)

  • 4 steps down from 9
  • −38% phone bookings
  • 1m 45s median time to book

What I learned

Testing with reception staff early surfaced constraints we would otherwise have missed, such as same-day emergency slots that must never appear online.
