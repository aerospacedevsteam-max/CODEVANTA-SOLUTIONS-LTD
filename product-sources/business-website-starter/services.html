FAQ

Questions about our services

Can I combine services?

Yes. Many clients start with strategy and continue into design and delivery. We will suggest a sensible order.

What do you need from us to start?

A short call, access to any existing materials and one main contact on your side.

Do you offer payment in stages?

Yes. We usually invoice per phase, so you only pay for work that has been agreed.

You receive every file and document. Ongoing support is optional and can be paused at any time.
