Features

Everything a busy team needs, nothing it doesn’t

Six building blocks that cover planning, delivery and reporting without a week of setup.

  • Flexible boards

    Switch between board, list and calendar views of the same tasks with one click.

  • Live progress

    Progress charts update as work moves, so status meetings start with the answers.

  • Shared workspaces

    Invite clients or contractors into one workspace without exposing the rest.

  • Automations

    Move cards, assign owners and post reminders automatically when conditions are met.

  • Granular permissions

    Decide who can view, comment or edit at workspace, project or task level.

  • Weekly digests

    A short summary of what moved, what is stuck and what is next lands every Monday.

How it works

From scattered requests to a clear plan

Collect every request in one inbox

Forms, email forwarding and quick-add turn incoming asks into tasks with an owner and a due date.

  • Custom intake forms per team
  • Duplicate detection
  • Priority labels that sort themselves

Plan the quarter on a living roadmap

Drag initiatives across weeks. Dependencies shift automatically, and everyone sees the same timeline.

  • Dependency lines with slack warnings
  • Milestones linked to tasks
  • Read-only share links for stakeholders

Report progress without a single spreadsheet

Status pages build themselves from task data. Add a comment, press share, done.

  • Burn-up and cycle-time charts
  • Scheduled email reports
  • CSV and JSON export
